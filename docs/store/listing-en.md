## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Sag-Aman is a scheduled ride service for children in Ashgabat:
kindergarten, school, after-school clubs. It is not a taxi. The same
vetted driver takes your child every day, and you meet them beforehand.

HOW IT WORKS

You set up a route: from, to, what time, which days. The dispatcher
assigns a driver from your circle — a regular one and one or two
backups, people you also know. After that the rides are created
automatically, and every morning you know who is coming.

HANDED OVER FROM ADULT TO ADULT

When picking your child up, the driver says your family code word. When
handing the child over at the kindergarten or school, the driver
confirms it with the institution code or the teacher signature. The
child confirms nothing — only adults do.

The teacher can also confirm receipt through a link, without installing
anything. That is a second confirmation, independent of the driver.

YOU WILL KNOW EVEN WITHOUT INTERNET

For the events that matter — picked up, handed over, cancelled, driver
replaced — an SMS arrives even when the in-app notification does not.
While the car is moving you can see it on the map.

MESSAGING WITHOUT PHONE NUMBERS

You can write to the driver inside the app, with ready-made phrases in
Russian, Turkmen and English. Phone numbers are never revealed, in
either direction. For anything urgent there is a button to call the
dispatcher.

SEVERAL CHILDREN IN ONE CAR

Neighbours and classmates can ride together, which costs less. Each
family has its own code word, and you never see other families children
in the app.

MONEY

The balance is topped up in cash through the driver or the dispatcher.
A ride is charged once it has actually happened, and every operation is
visible in the history.

LANGUAGES

Russian, Turkmen and English, including SMS.

ABOUT THE DRIVERS

Before driving, a candidate goes through document checks, a criminal
record certificate, an interview, and demonstrates how they install a
child seat. After that comes training and a test on the child handover
protocol. Without passing the test, no routes are assigned.

The service operates in Ashgabat.
```
