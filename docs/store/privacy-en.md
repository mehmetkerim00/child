# Sag-Aman Privacy Policy

Effective date: on publication.
Published at: `https://sagaman.tm/privacy/en`

Sag-Aman is a scheduled ride service for children in Ashgabat. This
policy is written in plain language, because parents read it, not
lawyers.

## What we collect

**About the child:** name, year of birth, the family code word, pickup
address and institution, whether a child seat is needed. Nothing else:
no documents, no photographs, no medical information.

**About the parent:** phone number (which is also the login) and name.

**About the driver:** name, phone, car details, hiring check results and
training results.

**About rides:** stage times (on the way, picked up, handed over), who
confirmed the handover, delays and their reasons.

**Car location:** only during an active ride — from "on the way" to
"handed over". Outside a ride no coordinates are recorded. The track is
kept for 30 days and then deleted automatically.

**Money:** top-ups and charges for rides.

We do not collect: your contacts, browsing history, advertising
identifiers, or data from other apps.

## Why

To get the child there, and to let the parent know where the child is.
Everything listed serves that directly:

- the code word — so the child is not handed to a stranger;
- location — so the parent can see the car on the map;
- the phone number — so a notification arrives by SMS without internet;
- ride and ledger history — so a disputed case can be examined.

## Who we share it with

Nobody for advertising, and nobody for sale. The data is visible to:

- **the parent** — only their own child and their own rides;
- **the driver** — only the children of their rides that day;
- **the institution** — the child name, time and driver name for today;
  it never sees family addresses or phone numbers;
- **the dispatcher and the service owner** — as far as the work requires.

In a pool (several children in one car) a parent sees neither the names
nor the addresses of other families children.

Technical transfers: SMS go through a mobile operator; in-app
notifications go through a push delivery service. If it is unavailable,
the service falls back to its own channel and SMS.

## Where the data is stored

On a server in Turkmenistan. Backups are encrypted.

## How long we keep it

- Ride track — 30 days.
- Rides, events and the ledger — while the family uses the service and
  for one year afterwards, for dispute resolution and accounting.
- Login codes — 5 minutes, and only their hash is stored.

## Parent rights

Write to the dispatcher and ask to:

- see what data about your family exists;
- correct anything wrong;
- delete your family and children from the service.

After deletion only anonymous accounting records remain (how many rides
and for what amount), with no names, addresses or phone numbers.

## Children

The app is used by adults. A child has no account, confirms nothing and
enters nothing. Data about a child is entered by the parent.

## Security

Passwords and tokens are stored only as hashes. Access is separated by
role and checked on the server. The app talks to the server over an
encrypted connection.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will say so in the app before the change
takes effect.

## Contact

Questions go to the dispatcher, in the app or by phone.
